# Data for the probability distributions lecture

The lecture reads these files from https://github.com/QuantEcon/data-lectures, where each
has a manifest recording its provenance. The copies here are byte-identical backups,
taken on 10 November 2026 at data-lectures commit `43f32e0e`.

- `us_adult_heights.csv`: standing height of US adults by sex, from the National Health
  and Nutrition Examination Survey (NHANES) 2015-2018, US National Center for Health
  Statistics. Public domain. Also used in the observed distributions lecture.
- `japan_population_by_age.csv`: population of Japan by single year of age, 2024, from
  the Population Estimates of the Statistics Bureau of Japan, under the Bureau's terms
  of use.
