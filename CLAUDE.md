# Python for Data Science: public book

Conventions for this book are kept in the private repo, alongside this one:

- `../py_for_ds_private/publishing.md`: how lectures are structured, written and
  published. Read its "Lecture structure" and "Writing style" sections before writing a
  lecture. Its "Build and deploy" section gives the commands for rebuilding the local
  site, which should happen after every change to the book.
- `../py_for_ds_private/my_syllabus.md`: the course philosophy and the
  session-by-session plan.

Everything in this repo is public. Test questions and assignment solutions stay in the
private repo.
