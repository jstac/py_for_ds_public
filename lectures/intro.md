# Python for Data Science

Lecture notes for Python for Data Science (DSC1030E / DSC6031E) at the National
Graduate Institute for Policy Studies, Winter 2027.

```{note}
These notes are under construction.
```

Each lecture can be downloaded as a Jupyter notebook. To use a notebook without
installing anything, open [Google Colab](https://colab.research.google.com/) and
choose File, then Upload notebook.

These notes are adapted from two QuantEcon lecture series by Thomas J. Sargent and
John Stachurski:
[A First Course in Quantitative Economics with Python](https://intro.quantecon.org/)
and
[Python Programming for Economics and Finance](https://python-programming.quantecon.org/).

[CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
